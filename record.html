Heard: narration recording

Temporary page for recording the films' narration. Your recordings stay in this browser until you download them. Nothing is sent to Heard's servers. Back to Heard

1. Microphone

Speak at your reading volume. The bar should move through the middle and stay out of the red end.

2. Read the line

How to read

Press Record, wait a beat, read the line, wait a beat, press Stop. The quiet at each end is trimmed later, so key clicks do no harm.
The bar under the line fills over the length of the current clip. It is a guide, not a target: the films are re-timed to your voice.
Read 911 as "nine one one". Keep the microphone in one place for the whole sitting, and finish all the lines in one sitting so the voice matches.
Record the room tone line last or first: ten seconds of sitting still, so the background noise can be measured.
